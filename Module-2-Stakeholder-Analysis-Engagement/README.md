# Module 2: Stakeholder Analysis

## 📌 Objective
To identify stakeholders, analyze their influence and interest, and define engagement strategies aligned with project goals.

## 📄 Project: Stakeholder Analysis & Engagement Plan (Completed)

### Work Done

#### Stakeholder Identification
- Patients
- Doctors and Nurses
- Administrative Staff
- IT Teams
- Hospital Leadership

#### Stakeholder Analysis
- Classified stakeholders using Influence vs Interest Matrix:
  - High Influence, High Interest
  - High Influence, Low Interest
  - Low Influence, High Interest
  - Low Influence, Low Interest

#### Stakeholder Engagement Planning
Defined:
- Communication methods (meetings, emails, dashboards, surveys)
- Frequency of interaction (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, as needed)
- Purpose (updates, feedback, decision-making)

## Skills Used
- Stakeholder analysis: To identify roles, challenges, and stakeholder expectations
- Decision-making: To categorize stakeholders and prioritize engagement strategies based on influence and interest
- Project documentation: To develop a clear and actionable Stakeholder Engagement Plan
- Strategic planning: To tailor a communication strategies to ensure stakeholder alignment and satisfaction

## 📌 Outcomes
- Stakeholder Matrix (Influence vs Interest classification)
- Stakeholder Analysis & Engagement Plan
- Defined communication strategies, engagement methods, and interaction frequency for each stakeholder group
