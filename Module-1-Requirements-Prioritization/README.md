# Requirements Gathering and Prioritization 

## 📌 Objective
To identify, analyze, and document business requirements for improving hospital operations and patient experience.

## 📄 Business Requirements Document (BRD)

### Work Done:
- Analyzed stakeholder profiles (patients, doctors, nurses, admin, IT)
- Identified key challenges:
  - Long patient wait times
  - Scheduling conflicts and overbooking
  - Communication gaps
  - Resource management issues
- Defined:
  - Problem statement
  - Business objectives
  - Functional and non-functional requirements
  - Constraints and assumptions
  - Acceptance criteria

### Data Analysis:
- Appointment data → identified scheduling conflicts
- Feedback data → linked low satisfaction to wait times and communication
- Resource data → identified shortages and availability issues

## 📄 Requirements Prioritization & RTM

### Work Done:
- Created a Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)
- Mapped requirements to business objectives
- Ensured alignment between requirements and expected outcomes

## Skills Used:
- Business analysis: I have structured a comprehensive Business BRD.
- Data analysis: I have identified trends and patterns in operational data to inform actionable business requirements.
- Problem-solving: I have addressed key challenges such as long wait times, resource inefficiencies, and communication gaps.
- Documentation and reporting: I have structured a well-organized BRD using a professional template.
- Critical thinking: You have prioritized requirements based on project goals and constraints.
  
- Requirements prioritization: I have applied the MoSCoW method to rank requirements
- Traceability and documentation: I have created a structured RTM to map requirements to project goals
- Analytical thinking: I have analyzed stakeholder needs and data insights to justify requirements
- Data validation: I have used Excel to validate requirements with real data evidence
- Decision-making: I have prioritized requirements based on feasibility and impact

## 📌 Outcome
A complete foundation for solution design based on structured requirements and data-driven insights.
